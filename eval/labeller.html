Sound level, 45 frames either side of the current frame (centre line). Each tick is one frame. The pop of a hit reaches the microphone about 5 frames after you see contact.

Near hit = a player on the camera's side of the net strikes the ball. Far hit = a player beyond the net. Mark the frame of contact, or your best guess if the ball is hidden. Bounce = the ball touches the floor. Serve = the serve is struck; it starts the point (mark the serve itself as a near or far hit too, one frame either side is fine). Point over = the moment the rally is dead (ball in the net, second bounce, out). Only what lies between a Serve and a Point over is scored, so ball-bouncing before the serve or after the point needs no marks. If the clip opens mid-point, leave out the Serve mark; if it ends mid-point, leave out Point over. Shift+←/→ jumps 10 frames, Backspace deletes the mark at or nearest the current frame.
